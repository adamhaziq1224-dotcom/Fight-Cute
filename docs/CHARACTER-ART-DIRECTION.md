# Cute character refinement — 25 September 2026

## Reference research

Reviewed the supplied climbing video and official Sanrio references:

- Hello Kitty official profile and design gallery: https://www.sanrio.co.jp/characters/hellokitty/
- Cinnamoroll official brand profile: https://corporate.sanrio.co.jp/en/business-info/brands/cinnamoroll/

Sanrio describes Cinnamon as quiet but friendly, born on a cloud, with large ears used for flying. This connects personality, silhouette, movement and setting. Hello Kitty's official profile describes a cheerful, kind personality with everyday hobbies. These are source facts, not animation specifications.

The following are our visual design interpretations of the supplied references, not rules claimed by Sanrio: broad heads, compact bodies, a quiet face with fewer marks, clearly recognizable silhouettes, restrained shading, and small purposeful gestures help the animals read at game scale. No external character artwork is bundled.

## Implemented

- Broader heads and shorter bodies, legs and resting arms across all seven in-game animals.
- Simple eyes and mouth, pastel cheeks, restrained underside shading instead of glossy sphere gradients.
- Rabbit blue facial marks and mint accessory; other animals retain their identifying ears, tail, beak, quills and colors.
- Challenge render scale increased from 0.47 to 0.68, without changing platform geometry or movement/collision physics.
- Coin collection triggers a short cheerful face and sparkles; landing compresses then settles the body.
- Challenge selection cards now use the same animated canvas rig as the playable character.
- Original articulated attack and ladder animations retained. Reduced-motion preferences disable decorative motion.

Battle selection still uses the existing separate SVG illustrations. This is a procedural 2D mascot refinement, not a claim of matching a studio-produced 3D animation pipeline.
